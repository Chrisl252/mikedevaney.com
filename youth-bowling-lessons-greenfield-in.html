4-Stage Youth Coaching Progression

  1. Stance & Footwork Timing: Establishing a balanced 4-step or 5-step approach with proper knee flex and smooth pushaway.
  2. Swing Line & Release Control: Developing a free, unforced armswing and consistent finger-and-thumb release for natural ball motion.
  3. Visual Targeting & Spare Systems: Teaching the 3-6-9 spare system to lock in high-percentage conversions on single pins (including 10-pins and 7-pins).
  4. Sport Patterns & Mental Preparation: Preparing competitive high school and Jr Gold athletes to read oil breakdown and adjust angles on sport oil patterns.

Why Parents Choose Private Instruction

Group youth leagues are great for recreational play, but individual instruction identifies the specific physical blockages holding your bowler back. Every lesson includes video highlights so parents and bowlers can clearly see swing improvements over time.

Mike also provides honest guidance on bowling gear, helping parents buy the right ball weight, surface, and layout while skipping unnecessary equipment purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What age should a bowler start private lessons?

    Bowlers can start private instruction as soon as they can comfortably hold and throw a ball (typically ages 7 and up). Building correct swing timing and stance early prevents bad habits that become harder to break later.

  • Do you coach high school varsity and Jr Gold tournament bowlers?

    Yes. Lessons cover high school varsity preparation, oil pattern transition strategies, mental game development, and Jr Gold sport pattern navigation for bowlers competing in Indiana High School Bowling (IHSB) and regional youth tournaments.

  • Where do youth lessons take place in Greenfield?

    All in-person youth lessons take place at Strike Force Lanes, located at 1539 W Main St in Greenfield, Indiana, serving Greenfield, New Palestine, Fortville, and East Indianapolis.
