### 1. The Rule of 31 Math
When playing on sport oil patterns, finding your target exit board at the end of the pattern uses the Rule of 31:
`Pattern Length (ft) - 31 = Breakpoint Board`
For example, on a 40-foot pattern, your ideal breakpoint exit is Board 9.

### 2. The 3-6-9 Spare System
Converting single pin spares requires eliminating oil hook variability:
- 10-Pin: Shift feet 9 boards LEFT, shoot cross-lane over the 3rd/center arrow with a plastic spare ball.
- 6-Pin: Shift feet 6 boards LEFT.
- 7-Pin: Shift feet 9 boards RIGHT, shoot cross-lane over the center arrow.
