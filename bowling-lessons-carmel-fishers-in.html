Private Instruction for North Indy Bowlers

Bowlers across Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, and Geist seeking tour-level instruction can train 1-on-1 with 2-time PBA Tour Champion Mike DeVaney at Strike Force Lanes in Greenfield, Indiana, located just 20 to 25 minutes down Olio Rd or Mt. Comfort Rd.

Mike brings 21 years of national PBA Tour experience and former Storm Products Head Coach expertise to youth varsity competitors and adult league bowlers alike.

Programs Offered

  • Youth & High School Coaching: Preparation for Hamilton County high school teams, IHSB matches, and Jr Gold qualifiers.
  • Adult League Average Elevation: Refining 4-step/5-step timing, release leverage, and spare conversion systems.
  • Equipment & Surface Matching: Professional assessment of ball surface, core dynamics, and layout fit.
