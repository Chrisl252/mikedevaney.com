How Virtual Coaching Works

Distance should not stop you from working on your game. With virtual coaching, you record video of your approach, release, and ball motion at your local bowling center and send it directly to Mike.

Mike breaks down your footwork, axis rotation, timing, and lane transition, providing a clear video response with exact drills to practice.

Simple 3-Step Process

  1. Record Your Approach: Take a 15-second video from directly behind your approach and a second video from the side.
  2. Text Your Video: Send your video directly to Mike at (702) 701-1494 with a brief note on what you are experiencing.
  3. Receive Breakdown & Action Plan: Mike reviews your swing mechanics and sends back a video breakdown with specific adjustments to make during your next practice session.
