Competitive High School Bowling in Central Indiana

High school bowling in Indiana (IHSB) has grown rapidly into a competitive varsity sport. Student athletes from Greenfield-Central, New Palestine, Mt. Vernon, Eastern Hancock, and East Indianapolis high schools compete for conference titles, sectional berths, and college bowling scholarships.

Former Storm Products Head Coach and 2-time PBA Champion Mike DeVaney provides 1-on-1 varsity coaching at Strike Force Lanes in Greenfield, IN.

4 Keys to Making the Varsity Roster

  1. Locking Down Spare Percentages: High school coaches value bowlers who do not throw away frames. Converting 90%+ of single pin spares is the first metric evaluated during tryouts.
  2. Baker Format Readiness: High school matches rely heavily on Baker team play. Developing anchor-leg composure and clean frames builds team confidence.
  3. Physical Consistency: Maintaining a balanced slide at the foul line and smooth armswing under match pressure.
  4. Equipment Preparation: Ensuring ball surface matches the house pattern at host centers.
