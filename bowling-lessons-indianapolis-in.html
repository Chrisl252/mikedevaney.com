Tour-Level Coaching Just Minutes East of Indy

Looking for professional bowling lessons in the Indianapolis area? Retired 2-time PBA Tour Champion Mike DeVaney offers private 1-on-1 bowling instruction at Strike Force Lanes in Greenfield, Indiana, located just 15 to 20 minutes east of I-465 on US-40 or I-70.

Whether you bowl in leagues at East Indianapolis centers, compete in high school tournaments, or are working to refine your fundamentals, Mike brings 21 years of PBA Tour experience to your game.

Serving Bowlers Across Central Indiana

  • East Indianapolis & Warren Township: Easy 15-minute drive east on Washington St (US-40).
  • Lawrence & Geist Area: Quick commute down Carroll Rd or Mt. Comfort Rd.
  • Beech Grove & Cumberland: Direct access via I-70 East to Greenfield exit 104.
  • Hancock County: Local coaching serving Greenfield, New Palestine, Fortville, and Shirley.

Coaching Tailored to Your Skill Level

Every bowler receives personalized instruction based on high-speed video analysis. From adult beginners learning footwork and timing to advanced scratch bowlers mastering oil pattern transition on sport shots, lessons focus on what actually moves your score.
